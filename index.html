About StudyShield

The Problem: Students often struggle to concentrate due to ambient noise and distractions. Finding a genuinely peaceful environment is critical for retention and productivity.

The Solution: StudyShield is an integrated platform designed to locate, evaluate, and recommend optimal study locations. We combine real-time acoustic analysis with curated geographic data to protect your focus.

Summary of Features

  • Interactive Mapping: Locate nearby libraries, parks, and quiet zones tailored for studying.
  • Acoustic Analysis: Use your device's microphone to calculate environmental noise via Root Mean Square (RMS) evaluation.
  • Quietness Scoring: Get standardized metrics to ensure your environment supports deep work.

Study Recommendations

  • High-Focus Tasks: Seek Green Zones (Libraries, enclosed study rooms). Target: < 45 dB.
  • Light Reading: Seek Orange Zones (Quiet cafes, calm parks, schools). Target: 45 - 60 dB.
  • Avoid: Red Zones (Markets, busy transit hubs). Noise exceeds 65 dB.
